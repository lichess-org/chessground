1) Multi-premove queue
Black is to move, so you (white) can queue a chain of premoves. Click or drag a white piece to a square to queue item 1, then another piece for item 2, etc. Each item is validated on a hypothetical board (earlier queued moves already applied). The most recently queued move is the brightest (yellow-green); earlier ones are muted red/orange.
2) L-shaped knight-move arrows
drawable.knightMoveBend is on. Arrows whose origin/destination are a knight's move apart are drawn as a two-segment right angle (origin → pivot → dest) with the arrowhead on the final leg. Non-knight arrows stay straight.
